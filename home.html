Organization Overview

Waseela Umeed is a humanitarian donation platform created to help people support orphanages, old age homes, disabled individuals, flood victims, health care projects, and emergency relief programs. Our mission is to connect donors with trusted causes and make donating simple, secure, and impactful for communities in need.
